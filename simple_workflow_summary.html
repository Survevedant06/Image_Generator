Summary of Today's Tasks & Modules

What We Built What It Does (Simple Language) How It Helps
Automatic Background Remover batch_remove_bg.py Automatically cuts the background out of product photos, leaving only the clean product on a transparent background. Sellers don't have to manually trace or erase product outlines in Photoshop.
Folder Auto-Watcher run_watcher.bat Sits quietly in the background monitoring your folder. The moment you drop a new picture in, it cuts out the background automatically. Zero human effort—just drag photos into the folder and finished cutouts appear on their own.
3D Multi-Angle Generator run_pipeline.py Takes one single photo of an object and uses AI to generate 6 different camera angles around it. Shoppers can view the product from multiple sides even if only one photo was taken.
Auto-Centering & Resizer standardize_views.py Detects the exact boundaries of the product, centers it perfectly, and resizes it onto a square canvas with uniform 10% margins. Makes all product listings look clean, high-end, and uniform like on Amazon or Shopify.
Seller Web Dashboard static/index.html A visual web interface where sellers can drag and drop photos, preview them on different background colors, and download them. Anyone can use the system inside a web browser without needing any programming knowledge.
Backend Web Server server.py The engine behind the website that receives uploads, manages processing queues, and sends finished images back. Ensures the website remains fast, smooth, and handles multiple image uploads reliably.
1-Click Desktop Launchers start_app.bat / run_watcher.bat Quick shortcut files you can double-click like normal software programs. Launches the studio or watcher without typing commands in the black terminal screen.
Phone & Photo Fixes Formats & EXIF Added support for iPhone photos (.heic) and automatically corrects sideways or upside-down pictures. No annoying errors when uploading photos taken directly from smartphones.

Why This Matters for Everyday Use

  • Saves Hours of Editing: Photos that previously required manual clipping paths now take seconds with zero human input.
  • Works Silently: You can leave the folder watcher running, take photos on your phone, paste them into the folder, and find transparent cutouts waiting for you.
  • Ready for E-Commerce: Standardized 1024x1024 square images match standard requirements for major e-commerce platforms.
