2. USER AUTHENTICATION & MULTI-TENANT WORKSPACES (Change #8)
================================================================================
The platform includes full user authentication and multi-tenant catalog
isolation. Each registered seller or merchant account maintains their own
private product catalog, isolated cutouts, custom scenes, and generation history.

A. Logging In:
   - When you open the website, the Authentication Modal appears automatically.
   - All accounts created in the database are displayed directly in the
     "Created Accounts" list on the card:
       * "alex"   (Alex Studio - Fashion, Beauty & Apparel)
       * "morgan" (Morgan Catalog - Tech, Tools & Electronics)
       * "vedant" (Vedant Surve - Studio Director & Admin)
       * Any newly registered or created merchant accounts
     Starter demo accounts use password: password123

   - Click "Sign In to Catalog" to access your private catalog.
   - To sign in with a custom username or email, click "Or sign in with custom
     username / email".

B. Registering a New Account:
   - Click the "Create Account" tab at the top of the modal.
   - Enter your desired Username, Full Name, Email address, and Password.
   - Click "Create My Catalog Account".
   - Your account is immediately created, saved permanently in the database,
     and will appear in the Created Accounts list whenever you return to sign in.

C. Switching Accounts & Logging Out:
   - Your active username is shown in the top-right header badge.
   - Click the logout icon next to your username to return to the sign-in screen
     at any time.

3. TEXT-TO-PRODUCT AI SCENE GENERATION (Change #5)
================================================================================
Sellers can now generate photorealistic, studio-quality commercial products
from scratch using pure text descriptions—no physical photography required!

A. How to Open the AI Generator:
   - Click the "AI Studio" [AI] button located in the top-right header, OR
   - Click the "Generate with AI" button on the Catalog Ingestion Card.

B. Generating a Product:
   1. In the "Product Description Prompt" box, describe your product in natural
      language (e.g., "Minimalist matte black ceramic pour-over coffee dripper").
   2. Select a Commercial Aesthetic Preset:
      - "Clean Studio": White infinity studio standard (Amazon/Shopify hero).
      - "Editorial Vogue": Dramatic, high-fashion luxury campaign lighting.
      - "White Hero": High-key pristine white background.
      - "Cyberpunk Neon": High-contrast noir with neon edge rim highlights.
      - "Minimalist Oak": Soft organic daylight with warm Scandinavian wood.
   3. (Optional) Provide a Custom Product Title or select a Target Category.
      If left blank, the system automatically infers the product title and
      classifies the category.
   4. Click "Generate & Ingest Product".

C. Automatic BiRefNet Pipeline:
   - The engine automatically renders the commercial visual plate.
   - It instantly routes the image through the BiRefNet neural matting pipeline
     to extract a pixel-perfect transparent PNG cutout.
   - The raw image, isolated cutout, SKU, tags, and category are saved directly
     into your authenticated seller catalog.

5. ARTIFICIAL SHADOW GENERATION SUITE (Change #1)
================================================================================
The artificial shadow engine solves the artificial "floating/sticker" look of
isolated cutouts by synthesizing physics-grounded ambient occlusion and
directional drop shadows.

A. Where to Find Shadow Controls:
   1. From the Catalog or Cutouts view, click on any product to open the "Product Studio".
   2. Switch to Tab 3: "Stylize & Presets".
   3. Scroll to the "Artificial Shadow Studio" control card on the left panel.

B. Key Shadow Controls & Sliders:
   1. Contact Shadow Toggle:
      - Simulates Ambient Occlusion (tight, dark contact patch directly under the
        base of the product where it touches the surface).
      - Anchors the product so it looks grounded and real.
   2. Drop / Cast Shadow Toggle:
      - Projects a directional cast shadow across the ground plane.
   3. 360-Degree Light Direction Slider (-180° to +180°):
      - Move the slider to change where light strikes the product.
      - A live badge shows the directional heading:
          * -180° = Left Light Source
          * -90°  = Overhead Top Light
          * -60°  = Classic Studio Top-Left Key Light (Recommended Default)
          * 0°    = Right Light Source
          * +90°  = Bottom Up-Lighting
   4. Shadow Distance Offset Slider (0px to 70px):
      - Controls the distance the cast shadow extends away from the product base.
   5. Blur Softness Slider (2px to 40px):
      - Controls Gaussian blur falloff (crisp vs. diffused lighting).
   6. Shadow Density / Opacity Slider (10% to 100%):
      - Tunes the opacity and subtlety of the shadow composite.

C. Applying Shadows:
   - Standalone: Click "Apply Shadow" at the top of the shadow card to preview
     the shadow directly on the transparent cutout.
   - In Scene Composite: Leave the toggles active and click "Render Plate" to
     fuse the shadow seamlessly with marble, podium, wood, or custom scenes.

6. ATMOSPHERIC COLOR TONE HARMONIZATION (Change #3)
================================================================================
When compositing a product photographed under warm yellow light onto a cool,
moody blue scene (or vice-versa), traditional cutouts look synthetic. The
Atmospheric Color Match engine harmonizes the product's color temperature with
the scene using statistical LAB color space transfer.

A. Where to Find Color Tone Matching:
   1. In the "Product Studio" modal, navigate to Tab 3: "Stylize & Presets".
   2. Locate the "Atmospheric Color Match" card underneath the shadow controls.

B. Adjusting Settings:
   1. "Auto-Adjust" Toggle:
      - Turn on to automatically compute the scene's ambient illumination and
        re-grade the product foreground to match.
   2. Atmosphere Blend Intensity Slider (10% to 100%):
      - Default is 70%.
      - Lower percentages (30%-50%) maintain strict brand/pantone fidelity while
        introducing subtle atmospheric color grading.
      - Higher percentages (70%-90%) blend the product deeply into high-contrast
        lighting environments (e.g. golden hour, dramatic sunset, moody neon).
   3. "Optical Edge Rim Bleed" Checkbox:
      - Simulates light scattering and rim reflection along the product borders
        where the background environment wraps around the subject.

C. Testing Standalone:
   - Click the "Match Tone" button to test color grading on the cutout before
     full-scene synthesis.

7. AI SUPER-RESOLUTION UPSCALE (2x / 4x TEXT-PRESERVING) (Change #2)
================================================================================
Product packaging, wine labels, perfume ingredients, and electronics labels often
get blurred or distorted by standard resizing. The text-preserving super-resolution
pipeline enhances resolution up to 8K while keeping typography, barcodes, and
fine edges tack-sharp.

A. How to Access AI Upscaling:
   - Method 1 (Export Tab):
     1. In "Product Studio", click Tab 4: "Commercial Export".
     2. Scroll to the "AI Upscaling Pass" section.
   - Method 2 (Catalog & Cutout Cards):
     1. Hover over any product card in the Catalog or Cutout grid.
     2. Click the quick upscale action.

B. Choosing the Scale Factor:
   - "1x Native": Standard unscaled export.
   - "2x Ultra-Sharp": Upscales up to ~4000x4000 px. Ideal for Amazon & Shopify
     high-res hover zoom.
   - "4x Master": Upscales up to ~8000x8000 px. Ultra-high fidelity for large
     format print lookbooks, packaging proofs, and billboards.

C. Text & Label Preservation Toggle:
   - Check "Preserve Typography & Label Sharpness" (Enabled by default).
   - This activates edge-directed contrast reinforcement on text characters and
     line art while smoothing flat surfaces without digital noise.

D. Running the Upscale:
   - Click "Quick Upscale" to generate and download the high-res file immediately, OR
   - Select your export preset (Amazon, Shopify, Reels, Web Banner) and click
     "Render & Download Asset".

8. CATALOG DUPLICATE DETECTION & SMART MERGING (Change #6)
================================================================================
Keep your product catalog clean, organized, and free of accidental double-uploads
or redundant image plates.

A. Automated Duplicate Alert Banner:
   - If identical or visually similar product photos are uploaded, an editorial
     alert banner appears at the top of the "Catalog & Products" tab:
     "Duplicate Items Detected [X DUPLICATES]".

B. Reviewing & Setting Sensitivity:
   1. Click "Review" on the alert banner to open the "Duplicate Catalog Manager".
   2. Choose your detection sensitivity in the subheader toolbar:
      - "Strict (95%+ / MD5)": Catches byte-for-byte exact matches and near-clones.
      - "Standard (88%+)": Balanced perceptual hash detection (Recommended).
      - "Broad (75%+)": Clusters different shots or angles of the same item.

C. Smart Merging vs. Purging:
   - "Smart Merge All":
     * Analyzes redundant product records and safely consolidates all transparent
       cutouts, 3D perspective angles, stylized scenes, and tags into the designated
       Primary Keep record.
     * Deletes redundant duplicate shell entries without losing any generated assets!
   - "Purge Duplicates":
     * Deletes duplicate product entries and their extra image files to instantly
       reclaim disk storage (shown in "Storage Reclaimable: X MB").

D. Upload Safeguard ("Auto-skip duplicates"):
   - On the main Catalog drop zone, check the "Auto-skip duplicates" box.
   - When dropping new batches of photos, the server performs a fast pre-flight
     visual check and automatically ignores files already present in your catalog.

11. RECOMMENDED END-TO-END SELLER WORKFLOW
================================================================================
Follow this 5-step workflow to generate commercial-ready catalog assets from zero:

Step 1: Ingest Product
   - Drag and drop your product photo onto the Ingestion Zone, OR
   - Tap "Camera" on your smartphone to shoot it live, OR
   - Tap "AI Studio" -> enhance your prompt -> click "Generate & Ingest Product".

Step 2: Inspect Cutout (Tab 1: Matting)
   - Open your product record.
   - Review the BiRefNet transparent cutout plate.
   - If needed, fine-tune the edge matting or re-process.

Step 3: Generate 360° Perspectives (Tab 2: 3D Perspectives)
   - Click "Synthesize 6 Perspectives".
   - The engine generates front, side, three-quarter, and elevated perspectives.
   - Export the 360° turntable GIF or download all angles as a ZIP.

Step 4: Stylize, Shadows & Lighting (Tab 3: Stylize & Presets)
   - Choose a scene preset (e.g. "Carrara Marble" or "Nordic Timber").
   - Enable "Contact Shadow" and "Drop Shadow", setting light direction to -60°.
   - Enable "Atmospheric Color Match" (70% blend) with Optical Edge Bleed.
   - Click "Render Plate".

Step 5: Commercial Export & Upscaling (Tab 4: Commercial Export)
   - Pick your marketplace standard:
       * "Amazon" (1:1, 2000px, JPG)
       * "Shopify" (1:1, WebP Master)
       * "Reels / Ad" (9:16 Vertical, 1080x1920, PNG)
       * "Web Banner" (16:9 Landscape, 1920x1080, WebP)
   - Select "2x Ultra-Sharp" or "4x Master" AI Upscaling.
   - Ensure "Preserve Typography & Label Sharpness" is checked.
   - Click "Render & Download Asset".
